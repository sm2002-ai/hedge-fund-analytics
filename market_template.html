Methodology & Disclosures
Scope This report screens the current S&P 500 constituent list for attractive long entry candidates using a blend of momentum, quality (Sharpe / low vol) and trend-following signals. It is a market-wide research memo, not a portfolio review.

Signals Momentum: 12-month total return skipping the most recent month (standard in academic momentum research). Volatility: annualized realized volatility over the trailing 126 trading days. Sharpe: annualized Sharpe ratio over the trailing 252 trading days (zero risk-free rate assumption). Trend: 50-day SMA divided by 200-day SMA minus 1. Positive readings indicate an uptrend. Max drawdown: largest peak-to-trough decline over the lookback window. Composite score: equal-weighted sum of z-scores, positive on momentum/Sharpe/trend, negative on volatility/drawdown magnitude.

Regime Classification Trend determined by SPY spot vs 50/200-day SMAs. Volatility regime determined by VIX level (low <15, normal 15–25, elevated 25–35, stress ≥35). Bias aggregates both: uptrend + low/normal vol = risk-on; downtrend or stress vol = risk-off; otherwise neutral.

Data Sources Price data: Yahoo Finance via yfinance. Universe: S&P 500 constituent list from Wikipedia (with static fallback for offline runs). Factor data: Kenneth French Data Library. Sector mapping: SPDR sector ETFs (XLK, XLF, XLV, XLY, XLP, XLE, XLI, XLU, XLB, XLRE, XLC).
