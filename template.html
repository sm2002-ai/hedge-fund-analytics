GIPS Disclosure & Performance Presentation This report is for informational purposes only and does not constitute an offer to buy or sell securities. Performance figures are based on {{ lookback_days }} trading days of historical data sourced from Yahoo Finance (yfinance). Returns are calculated on a daily total-return basis assuming no transaction costs or slippage. Past performance is not indicative of future results.

Risk Disclosures Value-at-Risk (VaR) estimates assume a normal distribution of returns. Historical simulation VaR uses the empirical return distribution. Conditional VaR (CVaR/Expected Shortfall) provides the expected loss beyond the VaR threshold. Factor exposures are estimated via OLS regression on Fama-French 5-factor data; statistical significance is indicated by p-values. Stress test scenarios replay historical crises and hypothetical shocks — they are not predictions of future performance.
